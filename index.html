About Dr. Vibha Shah

A Renowned
Ophthalmic Surgeon

Dr. Vibha Shah is an exceptionally distinguished eye surgeon based in Navsari, Gujarat. With an MS in Ophthalmology and over ten years of surgical mastery, she has transformed the vision — and lives — of thousands of patients across South Gujarat and beyond.

As the proud founder of Eye Care Clinic, she has created a dedicated sanctuary of Comprehnsive & paediatric eye care in Navsari.
